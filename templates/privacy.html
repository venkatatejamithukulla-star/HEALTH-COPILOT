🔒 Privacy, Security & Data Safety Policy

HealthMate AI is designed with privacy-by-default for Indian families, students, and seniors.

1. 100% Local SQLite Storage

All your medical reports, appointment schedules, and medicine reminders are stored in a local SQLite file (healthmate.db) on your own device. No health records are sent to third-party advertising companies or marketing trackers.

2. No Commercial Data Selling

We will never monetize, sell, or rent your health reports or personal details. The prototype uses fictitious sample data by default and does not require credit cards, passwords, or personal identity numbers.

3. Screen Privacy Mode

The application includes an instant "Privacy" button that automatically masks patient names on shared or public screens so bystanders cannot read family member names.

4. Educational Prototype Disclaimer

HealthMate AI is a coursework and hackathon educational prototype. It is NOT certified as a medical device and is NOT suitable for critical emergency treatment or legal medical records. Always consult a registered medical practitioner (MBBS/MD) for health diagnoses, prescriptions, and emergencies.

5. One-Click Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You can delete individual records anytime or clear all stored data with a single click using the Reset/Delete features.
