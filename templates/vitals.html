📖 Medical Reference Ranges (SPEC 12a)

Evidence-based physiological reference ranges compiled from AHA, WHO, and Indian clinical guidelines.

🩸 Blood Pressure (mmHg)

  • Optimal Normal: < 120 / < 80 mmHg
  • Elevated: 120–129 / < 80 mmHg
  • Stage 1 Hypertension: 130–139 or 80–89 mmHg
  • Stage 2 Hypertension: ≥ 140 or ≥ 90 mmHg
  • Hypertensive Crisis: ≥ 180 or ≥ 120 mmHg (Call 108)

🍬 Blood Glucose (mg/dL)

  • Hypoglycemia (Danger): < 70 mg/dL (15g sugar alert)
  • Normal Fasting: 70–99 mg/dL
  • Prediabetes Fasting: 100–125 mg/dL
  • Diabetes Fasting: ≥ 126 mg/dL
  • Normal Post-Meal: < 140 mg/dL (≥200 is diabetic)

🫀 Heart Rate & Oxygen

  • Normal Pulse: 60–100 bpm (Bradycardia <60, Tachycardia >100)
  • Severe Tachycardia: > 140 bpm (Emergency)
  • Optimal SpO2: 95–100%
  • Mild Hypoxia: 90–94%
  • Severe Hypoxia: < 90% (Call 108 Emergency)

🌡️ Temperature & BMI

  • Normal Body Temp: 97.0–99.0 °F
  • Fever: > 100.4 °F (> 103 °F is severe)
  • Normal BMI: 18.5–24.9 kg/m²
  • Overweight: 25.0–29.9 kg/m²
  • Obese: ≥ 30.0 kg/m²
