👨‍👩‍👧 Loved Ones & Family Support Guide

Guidance for families navigating a loved one's depression, anxiety, or addiction recovery.

✓ Helpful Do's

  • Listen without judgment, lecture, or emotional blaming.
  • Acknowledge small non-substance steps and celebrate effort.
  • Set clear, loving personal boundaries to avoid burnout.
  • Keep emergency and helpline numbers (108, 14446, 14416) saved.

✗ Avoid These Don'ts

  • Do NOT guilt-trip, shame, or dredge up past mistakes in crises.
  • Do NOT cover up, lie for, or financially enable active addiction.
  • Do NOT attempt forced, unsupervised cold-turkey detox at home.
  • Do NOT ignore your own therapy or support (attend Al-Anon).
